# Clinical Safety

## Non-negotiables

- Khong tu chan doan.
- Khong tu ke don.
- Khong tu thay doi lieu.
- Khong tu gui thong diep dieu tri ca the hoa.
- Khong thay the phan doan chuyen mon cua bac si.

## Red flag copy

Khi phat hien dau hieu nguy co cao, UI chi hien:

```text
Can bac si danh gia truc tiep ngay.
Khong su dung he thong nhu cong cu thay the cap cuu.
Khuyen nghi chuyen cap cuu hoac co so y te phu hop theo danh gia chuyen mon.
```

## Approval gates

- Care plan: draft -> pending approval -> approved/rejected.
- Risk level: suggested -> clinician confirmed.
- Handout A5: draft -> clinician approved -> printable official.
- AI output: generated draft -> reviewed -> accepted/edited/rejected/marked unsafe.

Can bac si kiem chung.
