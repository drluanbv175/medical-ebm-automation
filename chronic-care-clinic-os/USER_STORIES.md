# User Stories

## Bac si

- La bac si, toi muon xem danh sach nguoi benh nguy co do de uu tien danh gia truc tiep.
- La bac si, toi muon duyet care plan draft truoc khi tro thanh ke hoach chinh thuc.
- La bac si, toi muon xem xu huong sinh hieu, HbA1c, LDL-C va eGFR de theo doi muc tieu.
- La bac si, toi muon in loi dan A5 da duyet de nguoi benh mang ve.

## Dieu duong / care coordinator

- La dieu duong, toi muon xem danh sach can goi nhac va qua han tai kham.
- La dieu duong, toi muon cap nhat sinh hieu va checklist truoc kham.
- La care coordinator, toi muon tao task theo doi sau xuat vien va ghi ket qua lien he.

## Le tan

- La le tan, toi muon dat hen va check-in nguoi benh.
- La le tan, toi chi duoc xem thong tin hanh chinh can thiet.

## Duoc si cong tac

- La duoc si, toi muon xem danh muc thuoc va ghi khuyen nghi can bac si duyet.
- La duoc si, toi muon he thong canh bao trung hoat chat, trung nhom va da thuoc o muc can ra soat.

## Quan ly chat luong

- La quan ly chat luong, toi muon xem KPI tong hop da khu dinh danh.
- La quan ly chat luong, toi muon xuat CSV/XLSX tong hop khong co PII.

## Super Admin

- La super admin, toi muon quan ly nguoi dung, role, rule va audit log.
