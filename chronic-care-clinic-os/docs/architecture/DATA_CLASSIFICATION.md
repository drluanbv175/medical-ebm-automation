# Data Classification

## Patient Identity Data

- Full name.
- Phone.
- Address.
- Date of birth.
- Emergency contact.
- Patient identifiers.
- Patient contacts.

Access mac dinh bi gioi han theo role va organization/site.

## Clinical Coordination Data

- Chronic conditions.
- Medication list.
- Care goals.
- Care plan.
- Follow-up status.
- Risk status.
- Laboratory summary.
- Appointment status.
- Tasks.
- Communication logs.
- Referrals.

Chi hien thi theo need-to-know. Registry mac dinh khong hien dia chi, dien thoai, lien he than nhan hay toan bo ghi chu lam sang.

## Governance Data

- Clinical rules.
- Rule versions and approvals.
- Safety alerts.
- Incident reports.
- Clinical content versions and approvals.
- Audit logs.

Governance data can immutable audit va role rieng cho auditor.

## Reporting Data

- Quality metrics.
- Aggregated/de-identified dashboards.
- Monthly reports.

Khong dua PII vao dashboard trinh bay chung, analytics event hoac test snapshot.
