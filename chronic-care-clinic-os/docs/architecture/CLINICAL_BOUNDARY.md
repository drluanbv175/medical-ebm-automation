# Clinical Boundary

## Green Zone - duoc trien khai trong MVP

- Dat hen.
- Nhac lich.
- Registry benh man.
- Task dieu phoi.
- Checklist truoc kham.
- Theo doi muc tieu.
- Theo doi nguoi benh qua hen.
- Ra soat thuoc.
- Loi dan duoc bac si duyet.
- Dashboard chat luong.
- Theo doi sau xuat vien.
- Quan ly chuyen tuyen.
- Kho SOP va pathway.

## Yellow Zone - can phe duyet bac si truoc khi hanh dong

- Phan tang nguy co de xuat.
- Goi y ke hoach theo doi.
- Goi y checklist con thieu.
- Tom tat xet nghiem.
- Du thao loi dan.
- Du thao care plan.
- Du thao nhac nho nguoi benh.
- Du thao ghi chu kham.

Tat ca Yellow Zone phai co:

```text
Draft -> Nguoi tao -> Nguoi ra soat -> Nguoi duyet -> Thoi diem duyet -> Phien ban -> Nhat ky thay doi
```

## Red Zone - khong duoc tu dong hoa o MVP

- Tu chan doan.
- Tu ke don.
- Tu thay doi lieu.
- Tu ket luan chong chi dinh.
- Tu gui tu van dieu tri ca the hoa.
- Tu ky benh an.
- Tu tra loi tinh huong cap cuu.
- Tu gui ket qua xet nghiem bat thuong cho nguoi benh.
- Tu quyet dinh chuyen vien.
- Tu thuc hien kham benh tu xa.
- Tu thay the EMR phap ly.
