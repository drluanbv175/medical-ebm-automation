# SOP kham lan dau

## Muc tieu

Dam bao kham ban dau co du sinh hieu, sang loc co do, ra soat thuoc, phan tang nguy co, care plan va lich tai kham.

## Cac cong bat buoc

1. Chua co phan tang nguy co: khong hoan tat buoi kham.
2. Chua co lich tai kham hoac ly do khong hen: khong hoan tat buoi kham.
3. Co dau hieu nguy co cao: can bac si danh gia truc tiep ngay.

Can bac si kiem chung.
