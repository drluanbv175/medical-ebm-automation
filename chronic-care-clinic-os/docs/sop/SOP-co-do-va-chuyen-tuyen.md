# SOP co do va chuyen tuyen

## Tinh huong nguy co cao

Dau nguc cap, kho tho cap, dau than kinh khu tru, nghi dot quy, ngat, lu lan, giam y thuc, ha duong huyet nang, tang duong huyet kem mat nuoc/non/dau bung/roi loan y thuc, suy tim mat bu, xuat huyet bat thuong.

## Thong diep he thong

Can bac si danh gia truc tiep ngay. Khong su dung he thong nhu cong cu thay the cap cuu. Khuyen nghi chuyen cap cuu hoac co so y te phu hop theo danh gia chuyen mon.
