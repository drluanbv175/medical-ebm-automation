# SOP tiep nhan nguoi benh benh man

## Muc tieu

Tiep nhan dung nguoi benh, xac minh hanh chinh, dong thuan va ly do den kham.

## Buoc thuc hien

1. Xac minh ma ho so va thong tin lien he.
2. Kiem tra consent status.
3. Cap nhat nguoi lien he khan cap.
4. Tao hoac xac nhan lich hen.
5. Chuyen dieu duong do sinh hieu va checklist truoc kham.

## An toan

Khong nhap du lieu that vao moi truong demo. Khong xoa ho so lam sang.
