# Assumptions

- MVP chay local truoc, production chi sau khi bac si va don vi van hanh duyet clinical safety.
- Ban dau dung Auth.js-ready architecture, demo login role selector de tranh tao tai khoan that khi chua co quy trinh.
- UI demo dung seed data gia lap trong code; database schema va seed Prisma da san sang cho PostgreSQL.
- Khong tich hop SMS/Zalo/email that trong MVP; chi ghi communication log va reminder task.
- Khong tich hop AI production trong MVP; AI mac dinh disabled.
- Khong co ke don dien tu, thanh toan, bao hiem, ky so hoac telehealth.
- Cac muc tieu dieu tri trong seed chi de demo workflow, khong la khuyen cao ca the hoa.
- Cac rule nguy co trong MVP la ban nhap operational safety, can hoi dong/phu trach chuyen mon phe duyet truoc production.
