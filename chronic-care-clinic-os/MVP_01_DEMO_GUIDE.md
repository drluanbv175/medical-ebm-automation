# MVP-01 Demo Guide

1. Open `/mvp-01`.
2. Confirm the scope is only hypertension, type 2 diabetes and dyslipidemia.
3. Open `/patients` and select a fake patient.
4. Review risk suggestion and triggered rules.
5. Open `/visits/initial` to inspect required safety gates.
6. Open `/medications` to see medication reconciliation queue.
7. Open `/handouts` to view A5 draft language.
8. Open `/overdue` to see follow-up task queue.
9. Open doctor and nurse dashboards.
10. Open `/admin/audit` and `/admin/rules` to inspect governance surfaces.

Do not use with real patient data.
